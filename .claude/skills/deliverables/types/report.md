# Report

**Read by:** decision makers who need the outcome and the evidence, not the history.
**Inputs:** the verification report, the cycle's deltas, the closed tickets and the evidence they cite.

1. **Summary** - the outcome and the recommendation in a few sentences.
2. **Scope** - what was asked and what was covered.
3. **Findings** - what was found, each with the document or check that shows it.
4. **Results** - what passed, what failed and what was not checked.
5. **Risks and known issues** - what remains open and how serious it is.
6. **Next steps** - the actions proposed, with an owner for each.
