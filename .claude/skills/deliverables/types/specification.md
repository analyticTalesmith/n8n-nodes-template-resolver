# Specification

**Read by:** builders and reviewers who need to know what the product must do.
**Inputs:** the product vision, the feature modules, the acceptance scenarios, the data model and the decisions.

1. **Scope** - what is in and what is out.
2. **Requirements** - each capability as a testable statement, grouped by module.
3. **Acceptance scenarios** - the given, when, then cases that prove each requirement.
4. **Constraints** - limits on the stack, the data and the behavior that the design must respect.
5. **Decisions** - the choices made and the reason for each.
6. **Open questions** - what is not settled yet and who decides.
